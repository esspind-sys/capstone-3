IT Support Policy. All IT issues must be logged as a ticket in the service desk portal; requests by email or chat are not tracked. Every ticket is assigned a severity. Critical tickets, such as a client-facing system outage or a suspected security breach, must be resolved within 24 hours of being opened. High severity tickets should be resolved within 48 hours. Low severity tickets are addressed on a best-effort basis with no strict deadline. If a critical ticket is not resolved within 12 hours, it must be escalated to the on-call engineering lead. If a critical ticket remains unresolved at the 24-hour mark, it is automatically escalated to the department director. Lost or stolen laptops must be reported within one hour so the device can be remotely wiped. Password resets are self-service. Consultants must never install unapproved software on firm or client devices.
