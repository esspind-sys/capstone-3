Project Governance Policy. Every project has one of three statuses: active, on-hold, or completed. Active projects send the client a written status report every week and hold a steering committee meeting every month. A project may be placed on hold only with written agreement from the client sponsor. Any project on hold for more than 30 days requires a steering committee review to decide whether to resume, re-scope, or close it, and consultants assigned to an on-hold project should be released to other work within two weeks. Spending more than 10 percent over a project's approved budget requires approval from the engagement director before further costs are incurred. A project is marked completed only after the client signs the acceptance document and all invoices are issued. Every completed project receives a lessons-learned review within 30 days of closure, and the findings are shared with the delivery practice.
