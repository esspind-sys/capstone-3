Consultant Onboarding Process. Every new consultant completes a structured two-week onboarding programme before joining a client engagement. Day 1 covers HR induction, laptop and account setup by IT, security awareness training, and signing the code of conduct and confidentiality agreement. Days 2 and 3 cover methodology training: the firm's delivery framework, quality standards, and time-entry rules. Consultants must record billable hours in the time system every working day. Days 4 and 5 are spent shadowing a Senior consultant or Manager on an active project. Week 2 is role-specific training by specialisation, such as data engineering, analytics, strategy, or healthcare systems, plus completion of the independence and conflict-of-interest declaration. Each new consultant is assigned an onboarding buddy at Senior grade or above for their first 90 days. A consultant may not be staffed on a billable project until their line manager has signed off onboarding. HR reviews each new consultant's progress at 30, 60, and 90 days.
