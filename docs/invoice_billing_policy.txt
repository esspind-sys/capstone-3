Invoice and Billing Policy. Invoices are issued at each agreed project milestone and carry payment terms of 30 days from the invoice date. An invoice has one of three statuses: pending, paid, or overdue. An unpaid invoice becomes overdue the day after its due date. The overdue process is as follows. On day 1 overdue, finance sends the client an automated reminder. At 15 days overdue, the project manager contacts the client directly. At 30 days overdue, the invoice is escalated to the engagement director, who may pause further non-critical work on the project. At 60 days overdue, the matter is referred to the legal team. Pending invoices are reviewed weekly in the finance operations meeting. Any write-off of an invoice requires approval from the Chief Financial Officer. Consultants must not discuss payment disputes with client staff; all billing queries go through the project manager.
